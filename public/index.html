Method, scoring & payment accounting

Questions were discovered through social search and inspected before testing. Inputs include extracted assertions and explicit inference probes. The original 24-input manifest was frozen first; six different supplemental claims were frozen afterward. Failed attempts remain included.

19 of 21 completed assessments matched the author-assigned provisional reference labels; two did not. This is diagnostic label agreement, not independently judged accuracy. The iPhone result explained unresolved evidence but labelled it mixed; another result labelled an unverified leak refuted. One assessment included a grounding notice. Quote matching tests provenance, not truth or entailment.

Seven completed checks had retrieval warnings. Nine attempts returned no usable result. Chain audit confirms 27 payments totalling $1.35: 21 returned assessments, six settled without a usable captured response, and three attempts did not settle. Transaction signatures are published in the dataset. Read each source, reference basis, and limitation.

These are current community-derived capability tests, not equal-budget, same-time head-to-head trials against Grok. Some bot replies already contain the correct distinction. Full text, bot excerpts, inputs, expected labels and outcomes are published together.
